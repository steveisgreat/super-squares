# 002 — Web app first: no native apps before 2027-02-14

**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** accepted · **Roadmap:** Later

## Context
The goal is to sell Super-Squares publicly in time for the NFL championship game on
2027-02-14. The product already runs in any browser, on a phone, on a laptop, or
mirrored to a TV with AirPlay or a smart-TV browser.

## Decision
Ship a hosted web app only. No native iOS, Android or Apple TV app before 2027-02-14.
Phone wrappers (Capacitor) for App Store and Google Play discovery are reconsidered
after that date (roadmap "Later"); check the App Store real-money gaming guideline
first. Apple TV is not planned.

## Consequences
All launch effort goes into the web app, hosting and go-to-market. Nothing waits on
store review. Discovery comes from search and sharing, not app stores.
