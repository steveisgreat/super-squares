# 005 — No NFL marks

**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** accepted; the logo rule is replaced by [013](013-team-logos-restored.md) (2026-11-24) · **Roadmap:** R4, R12

## Decision
No NFL team logos anywhere in the product, and marketing says "the Big Game", never
"Super Bowl". Team names, abbreviations and colors stay.

## Consequences
The ESPN logo CDN is dropped (R4); teams show a generic badge in their colors. Landing
and search pages (R12) use "football squares generator" and "Big Game squares template".
A trademark check on the name "Super-Squares" is still open (roadmap open question 4).
