This policy explains what [PRODUCT NAME], operated by [COMPANY], collects and does today, and what is planned. It describes how the software actually works. The section marked for review covers features that are not live yet.

1. What we store

  • Game data. When a host creates a game, we store its settings (team names, scoring mode, dates), the grid, and the names the host types onto squares and into the player list. We also store a "paid / unpaid" flag per player that the host sets for their own tracking, plus scores and winners.
  • Nothing else about players. Players do not create accounts, and we do not ask for or store email addresses, phone numbers or payment details today. Names are whatever the host chooses to type, so hosts should use first names or nicknames.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider processes ordinary request information (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site.

2. Where it is stored

The site is hosted on Vercel, and game data is kept in a Postgres database run by Neon. These providers process data for us. [HOSTING REGIONS — confirm.]

3. Cookies and device storage

  • Host session cookie (sbs_host). When a host signs in, we set this cookie so they stay signed in for up to 30 days. It is HttpOnly, so page scripts cannot read it, and it holds a signed expiry value, not personal data. People who only view boards do not get it.
  • Player view memory (browser localStorage). On a board's player page, your browser remembers which name you picked from the list so it is preselected next time. It stays on your device, is not sent to us, and you can clear it in your browser settings.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

4. Third parties

  • Google Fonts. The app loads fonts from Google (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), so your browser sends Google your IP address and standard request details. [Consider self-hosting fonts — for review.]
  • Live scores. Score lookups are made by our server, not your browser, so your device does not contact the score provider. Only public game and team information is requested; no player names are sent.
  • Hosting providers. Vercel and Neon, as above.
  • We do not sell personal information, show ads or run analytics today.

5. Who can see a board

Anyone who has a board's link (for example from its QR code) can view it, including every name and the paid / unpaid flags on it. The link is not a password. Hosts should share it only with people they are happy to have see those names, and should not type sensitive information onto a board. Managing games requires the host to sign in.

6. How long we keep data, and deleting it

A host can delete a game at any time from the app, which removes its stored data. [RETENTION PERIOD for abandoned or finished games and for backups — to be decided; today games are kept until deleted.] To ask us to delete a game or a name, use the Contact page.

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and hosts must be at least 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a name of a child appears on a board and you want it removed, contact us.
